RietveldTV, in your browser.

Interactive demo. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you set a weather city. The apps here are placeholders; the real launcher shows your own apps.

CITY

ENTER TO CONFIRM · ESC TO CANCEL
SETS LIVE WEATHER: THE CITY NAME IS SENT TO OPEN-METEO.COM. WITHOUT A CITY THE DEMO USES SAMPLE DATA.

This demo uses placeholder icons (no brand logos). On a real TV the launcher draws your installed apps’ own logos, or, with Rietveld logos on, abstract red, blue and yellow stand-ins for them.
